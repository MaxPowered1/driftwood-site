What it does

Paperwork in, a maintenance record out.

🔍

Everything is evidence-backed

Every proposed event shows the document's own words beside it, and every answer names the page it came from. If a claim isn't supported by a passage you can read, you'll see that before you accept it.

📷

No scanner needed

Point the camera at a paper invoice. Steward straightens the page, reads the text, and files it with the thing it belongs to. Photos and files import the same way.

🔔

Maintenance that arrives on time

Set a service interval and Steward tracks what's due, reminds you the morning it is, and records the work when you mark it done. See it as a list or a month at a glance.

🛡️

Warranties, before they lapse

Give a component its expiry date and Steward warns you a month ahead — while there is still time to book a repair somebody else pays for.

A look inside

A house, its components, and what needs doing.

Private by default

Your documents stay on your devices and sync through your own iCloud account. There is no Steward server and no account to create. Answers are written on-device where your hardware supports it; sending a question to Claude instead is opt-in, uses your own API key, and is never switched on for you.
