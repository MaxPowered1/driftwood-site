Privacy Policy

Steward Records · Effective 21 November 2026

In short: Steward keeps your documents and records on your own device and in your own iCloud account. There is no Steward account and no Steward server, so we do not receive, store or see any of it. Two optional features can send document text away to be read, and both are off until you switch them on. We run no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The sections below say the same thing in the detail that data-protection law expects.

1. Who this policy is from

Steward Records (“Steward”, “the app”) is published by Driftwood Apps, a trading name of Robert Bollinger, an individual developer (“we”, “us”, “our”). You can reach us at help@driftwoodapps.com.

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we are the data controller for the limited processing described in section 5. For everything you put into the app we are neither controller nor processor in any practical sense, because the data never reaches us. We have not appointed a data protection officer; the scale of our processing does not require one.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Steward Records app on iPhone and iPad, and the website at driftwoodapps.com. It does not cover Apple’s App Store or iCloud, Anthropic’s services, or any other company’s product, each of which has its own privacy policy and its own relationship with you.

3. What Steward handles, and where it lives

Using Steward produces the following categories of information:

  • Documents you import — PDFs, photographs, scans, and CSV or text files.
  • Text extracted from them, and any corrections you make to that text.
  • Records you accept — dates, costs, vendors, component names, model and serial numbers, warranty dates, and the passage and page each came from.
  • Reminders and schedules you set.
  • App settings, including whether each optional off-device feature is switched on.
  • An Anthropic API key, if you choose to supply one.

All of it is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud it also syncs between your own devices through your private iCloud database, which is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and which we cannot access. An API key you supply is held in your device’s keychain, marked so that it does not sync to iCloud or to your other devices.

Documents about things you own can contain information about you or about other people — an address, a contractor’s name, a price. Steward treats that text as yours. It is never sent to us, and it goes elsewhere only in the circumstances in section 4.

4. Optional: having a document read somewhere other than your device

By default Steward reads documents and answers questions on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere. Some documents are longer than the model on your device can read. For those, Steward offers two choices. Both are off until you switch them on, each behind its own consent screen, and neither is needed to use the app.

4.1 Apple’s Private Cloud Compute

On iOS 27 and later you may turn on Apple’s Private Cloud Compute. When it is on and you ask a question, your question and the relevant text — which, for a question about a single document, may be the whole of that document’s text — are sent to Apple to be read, through Apple’s own system frameworks. That text is handled by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. It is not sent to us and we never see it. Steward keeps no copy and sends nothing else: no name, no account, no device identifier.

Deciding what a document is never uses this feature; that runs on your device. You can turn the feature off at any time in Settings, which stops it entirely. The setting applies to one device and is not synced.

4.2 Anthropic’s Claude

You may instead supply your own Anthropic API key. When the feature is on and you ask a question, or ask a document to be read, the relevant text from that document and your question are sent to Anthropic’s API under your account with Anthropic, not ours. That text is subject to Anthropic’s privacy policy and your agreement with them. Anthropic bills you directly; we never see your usage or your costs. Steward keeps no copy and sends nothing else — no name, no account, no device identifier.

You can remove your API key at any time in Settings, which stops this entirely.

4.3 Turning both off

Setting Steward to never leave this device switches both features off at once. Switching a feature off takes effect immediately; it cannot undo a request already made while it was on.

5. The only information we actually receive

We operate no servers and no account system, so the app sends us nothing. The only ways personal data reaches us are:

  • If you email us. We receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to attach.
  • Aggregated reports from Apple. Apple gives developers aggregated, non-identifying statistics about downloads, sales and crashes, and sales and payment reports. These do not identify you to us. Apple is the seller of record for every purchase, and we never see your payment details.
  • App Store reviews you choose to write, which are public and carry whatever name you have given Apple.

We do not track you across apps or websites. Steward contains no analytics, advertising or attribution software, reads no device identifiers, and shows no advertising. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.

6. Legal bases for processing (UK and EU GDPR)

  • Answering your support email — our legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Sending document text off your device — your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), given on the feature’s consent screen and withdrawable at any time in Settings. Withdrawing it does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Everything the app does on your device — no legal basis is needed from us, because we are not processing it. It is your own data on your own device.

7. How long things are kept

  • On your device and in your iCloud: until you delete them. Nothing expires on its own.
  • Support correspondence: kept while we deal with your question and for up to 24 months afterwards, so that we can recognise a recurring problem, then deleted.
  • Apple’s aggregated reports: retained for normal business and tax purposes. They do not identify you.

8. International transfers

We transfer nothing internationally, because we receive nothing to transfer. If you switch on one of the optional features in section 4, the text you send is processed on infrastructure operated by Apple or by Anthropic, which may be outside your own country. Those transfers happen under each company’s own safeguards and terms, not ours.

9. Your rights in the UK and the EEA

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent you have given.

In practice you exercise most of these yourself without asking us, because we hold nothing: your records are in the app, where you can read, correct, export and delete them at any time. The app exports everything it holds as a report, a spreadsheet or a JSON file, which is what portability means here.

For the one category we do hold — email you have sent us — write to help@driftwoodapps.com and we will respond within one month. We may ask you to confirm the address you wrote from, which is the only way we can identify your correspondence.

If you think we have handled your data badly you may complain to your national supervisory authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner’s Office; in the EEA it is the authority for the country you live in.

10. Notice for California residents

Under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, and covering the preceding twelve months:

  • The only category of personal information we have collected is identifiers — an email address — and only from people who chose to email us. We collect it to answer them.
  • We have not sold personal information and have not shared it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not do either, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • We collect no sensitive personal information and run no financial incentive programmes.

You have the right to know, to delete, to correct, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. Write to help@driftwoodapps.com. An authorised agent may act for you with your written permission.

11. Notice for other United States residents

If you live in a state with a comprehensive privacy law — Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and others — you have broadly the same rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and appeal. We do not sell personal data, use it for targeted advertising, or profile you. Use the same address above, and if we refuse a request you may appeal by replying to our answer.

12. Children

Steward is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and in fact we collect nothing from the app at all. If you believe a child has sent us something by email, write to us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Your records are protected by your device: by its passcode, by iOS file protection and, if you use iCloud, by your Apple Account and whatever protections you have enabled on it. An API key you supply is stored in the device keychain, available only once the device is unlocked, and deliberately not synced to iCloud. We hold no copy of anything, which removes the most common way personal data is lost.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information you send us by email.

14. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered locally by your device. No reminder content is sent anywhere, and we never see that you have one.

15. Camera and photos

Steward can photograph or scan a document, and can read a photo you choose. Those images are imported into the app on your device and handled exactly like any other document. They are not sent anywhere, and Steward does not browse your photo library — you choose what it sees.

16. Deleting your data

Deleting a subject in the app removes it and its records. Deleting the app removes everything it stored locally; anything already in your iCloud account can be removed from iCloud settings on your device. Because we hold no copy, deleting it on your side deletes it altogether.

17. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the effective date at the top of this page and publish the new version here. Where a change is significant — a new place your data can go, for instance — we will also say so in the release notes of the version that introduces it, as we did when Apple’s Private Cloud Compute was added.
