Privacy Policy

DriveTwin · Effective 21 November 2026

In short: DriveTwin runs entirely on your Mac. It copies your files between your external drive and your own iCloud Drive, using Apple’s own system. There is no DriveTwin account, no DriveTwin server and no license check, so we never receive, store or see your files, your file names or anything about them. We run no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The sections below say the same thing in the detail that data-protection law expects.

1. Who this policy is from

DriveTwin (“DriveTwin”, “the app”) is published by Driftwood Apps, a trading name of Robert Bollinger, an individual developer (“we”, “us”, “our”). You can reach us at hello@driftwoodapps.com.

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we are the data controller for the limited processing described in section 5. For everything you sync with the app we are neither controller nor processor in any practical sense, because the data never reaches us. We have not appointed a data protection officer; the scale of our processing does not require one.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the DriveTwin app for macOS, and the website at driftwoodapps.com. It does not cover Apple’s iCloud, Gumroad’s store, or any other company’s product, each of which has its own privacy policy and its own relationship with you.

3. What DriveTwin handles, and where it lives

To do its job, DriveTwin works with the following information, all of it on your Mac:

  • The two folders you choose — a folder on your external drive and a folder in your iCloud Drive — stored as their paths and as security “bookmarks” so the app can reopen them after a restart.
  • A local record of what it has synced — the names, sizes and modification dates of the files in those two folders, kept in a small database on your Mac so the app can tell what has changed since last time.
  • Your settings — the schedule, the sync direction (two-way or upload-only), whether deletions are mirrored, the free-space limit, and which items to skip.
  • The files themselves, while they are being copied between your external drive and your iCloud Drive.

None of this is sent to us. The settings and the sync record stay on your Mac. The files move between your external drive and your own iCloud Drive and nowhere else — see section 4.

DriveTwin is not sandboxed, because it needs to reach an external drive and an iCloud folder that you pick. It only ever touches the two folders you choose and the places macOS itself keeps its settings; it does not read the rest of your disk, and it does not browse your files beyond those folders.

4. iCloud: the files go into your own account

DriveTwin’s whole purpose is to keep a copy of your drive in your iCloud Drive. It does this through Apple’s own system interfaces: it asks macOS to upload a file to iCloud, waits for Apple to confirm it is safely stored, and then releases the local copy to free up space. The transfer is between your Mac and your Apple Account.

Your files in iCloud are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and are held under your Apple Account, which we cannot access. DriveTwin never sends your files, your file names, or any copy of them to us or to any third party. We operate no storage of our own and we receive none of your content.

5. The only information we actually receive

DriveTwin has no account system, no server and no license or activation check, so the app itself sends us nothing. The only ways personal data reaches us are:

  • If you email us. We receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to attach.
  • Purchase information from Gumroad. DriveTwin is sold through Gumroad, which is the seller of record. When you buy, Gumroad collects your payment and gives us limited order information — your email address, and sometimes your name and country — so that we can deliver the app, provide support and meet tax obligations. We never see your full card or payment details. Gumroad handles your payment under Gumroad’s privacy policy.

We do not track you across apps or websites. DriveTwin contains no analytics, advertising or attribution software, reads no advertising identifiers, and shows no advertising. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.

6. Legal bases for processing (UK and EU GDPR)

  • Answering your support email — our legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Handling your purchase — performance of our contract to sell you the app, and our legitimate interests in keeping proper business records (Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • Everything the app does on your Mac and in your iCloud — no legal basis is needed from us, because we are not processing it. It is your own data on your own device and in your own account.

7. How long things are kept

  • On your Mac and in your iCloud: until you delete them. Nothing expires on its own.
  • Support correspondence: kept while we deal with your question and for up to 24 months afterwards, so that we can recognise a recurring problem, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: retained for as long as normal business and tax law require.

8. International transfers

We receive so little that we transfer almost nothing internationally. Your support email and the order information from Gumroad may be processed on infrastructure outside your own country — for example by our email provider or by Gumroad. Those transfers happen under each provider’s own safeguards and terms. The files you sync are not part of this: they move between your Mac and your own iCloud account, under Apple’s terms, not ours.

9. Your rights in the UK and the EEA

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent you have given.

In practice you exercise most of these yourself without asking us, because we hold almost nothing: your files and sync records are on your Mac and in your iCloud, where you can read, change and delete them at any time. For the little we do hold — email you have sent us, and your order information — write to hello@driftwoodapps.com and we will respond within one month. We may ask you to confirm the address you wrote from or bought with, which is the only way we can identify your records.

If you think we have handled your data badly you may complain to your national supervisory authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner’s Office; in the EEA it is the authority for the country you live in.

10. Notice for California residents

Under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, and covering the preceding twelve months:

  • The only category of personal information we collect is identifiers — an email address, and sometimes a name and country — from people who email us or who buy the app. We collect it to answer support and to fulfil and record purchases.
  • We have not sold personal information and have not shared it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not do either, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • We collect no sensitive personal information and run no financial incentive programmes.

You have the right to know, to delete, to correct, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. Write to hello@driftwoodapps.com. An authorised agent may act for you with your written permission.

11. Notice for other United States residents

If you live in a state with a comprehensive privacy law — Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and others — you have broadly the same rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and appeal. We do not sell personal data, use it for targeted advertising, or profile you. Use the same address above, and if we refuse a request you may appeal by replying to our answer.

12. Children

DriveTwin is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and in fact we collect nothing from the app at all. If you believe a child has sent us something by email, write to us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Your files are protected by your Mac — by its password and FileVault if you use it — and, once in iCloud, by your Apple Account and whatever protections you have enabled on it. DriveTwin reaches only the two folders you choose, holds its settings in your Mac’s standard preferences, and keeps no copy of anything off your device, which removes the most common way personal data is lost. DriveTwin is distributed outside the Mac App Store, signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so macOS can verify it has not been tampered with.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information you send us by email.

14. Notifications and background activity

DriveTwin can show notifications — that a sync finished, or that something needs your attention — and these are created and delivered locally by your Mac. It can also start at login and watch your folders for changes so it can sync automatically. All of this happens on your Mac. No notification, schedule or activity is sent anywhere, and we never see any of it.

15. Deleting your data

Removing DriveTwin removes its settings and its sync record from your Mac. Your files are not touched by uninstalling: the copies on your external drive and in your iCloud Drive remain yours, and you manage them from Finder and from iCloud settings as usual. Because we hold no copy of any of it, deleting it on your side deletes it altogether. For the order information we received through Gumroad, email us and we will delete what we are not required by law to keep.

16. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the effective date at the top of this page and publish the new version here. Where a change is significant — a new place your data could go, for instance — we will also say so in the release notes of the version that introduces it.
